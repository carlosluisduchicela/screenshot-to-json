screenshot-to-json

Screenshot in, structured record out. Paste the text from a save — or OCR an image in your browser — and get clean JSON.

No server, no server-side LLM. OCR is optional and runs in your browser; pasting text works fully offline. Extraction is heuristic and will miss things — see the notes under the output.

1. Image (optional)

OCR loads Tesseract.js from a CDN only when you click the button. If it can’t load, you’ll get a clear message and can paste text instead.
